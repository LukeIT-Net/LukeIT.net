Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27th, 2026

Last Updated: October 27th, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LukeIT ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our website at lukeit.net (the "Website") and our applications distributed through the Microsoft Store and GitHub (the "Apps").

1. Summary

The Apps and the Website do not collect personal information from you directly or ask you to create an account, and we do not use advertising or tracking tools. Our Apps contain no analytics of their own, and our Website uses only the privacy-focused analytics described in Section 4. The only information we receive about our Apps comes from Microsoft (through the Microsoft Store and Microsoft Partner Center) and GitHub, as described below. Other separate services connected through our domain are covered by their own privacy policies.

2. Information we receive through the Microsoft Store and GitHub

When you download or use our Apps through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft collects certain information as part of operating the Store. We access limited reports about our Apps through Microsoft Partner Center. These reports may include:

  • Acquisition and installation data: how many people viewed, downloaded, or installed an App, broken down by market (country or region), device type, and acquisition channel.
  • Usage data: aggregated information about how the App is used on Windows devices, which may include custom events if our App has them.
  • Health and crash data: information about App performance and reliability, such as crashes and unresponsive events, which may include technical details like the Windows version and device type.
  • Ratings and reviews: any rating or review you choose to submit in the Microsoft Store, which Microsoft may display publicly along with the display name you use.
This information is generally provided to us in aggregated or de-identified form. We use it only to understand how our Apps are performing, fix bugs, improve the Apps, and respond to reviews. We do not attempt to identify individual users from it.

Microsoft is responsible for the information it collects directly from you, including purchase and payment details. That information is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive your payment card details.

When you download our Apps from GitHub, GitHub collects information as part of operating its service. As the owner of our repository, we can see limited aggregated information, such as how many times a release file was downloaded and repository traffic statistics like page views, clones, and referring sites. We do not use this to identify individual users.

If you open an issue, post a comment, or otherwise interact with our GitHub repository, your GitHub username and anything you write will be visible to us and to the public. Please do not post personal or sensitive information there. GitHub is responsible for the information it collects directly from you, which is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following from our Apps or Website:

  • Names, email addresses, or other contact details
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, files, or other content on your device
  • Advertising identifiers

4. Website

Our Website is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. ("Cloudflare"). We do not use advertising or marketing trackers, and we do not set our own cookies. We use two services that give us aggregated information about visitors, Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Search Console, both described below. We do not directly collect personal information from visitors.

To deliver and protect the Website, Cloudflare automatically processes technical information about each request, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and the pages requested. Cloudflare may also set strictly necessary security cookies, for example to block malicious traffic and bots. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you and we do not use it to identify visitors. Cloudflare handles this information under its own Privacy Policy.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit our Website and how well it performs. It measures things like pages viewed, the referring site, the visitor's country, browser and device type, and page load times. According to Cloudflare, this service does not use cookies or local storage, does not build profiles of individual visitors, and does not track people across sites or over time. We use it only for aggregated statistics.

We use Google Search Console to understand how our Website appears in Google Search. It shows us aggregated information, such as which search queries led to our pages, how often our pages appeared and were clicked, and the countries and device types involved. It does not tell us who individual visitors are, and it does not place anything on our Website. We verified ownership of our Website using a DNS record, which adds nothing to the pages visitors see. Google collects and processes information about people who use Google Search under Google's Privacy Policy.

5. How we use information

We use the information described in Section 2 only to:

  • Understand how many people use our Apps and visit our Website, and how they perform
  • Diagnose and fix crashes and technical problems
  • Improve our Apps and plan future updates
  • Respond to reviews and customer feedback
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may disclose information only if required to do so by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property. Microsoft (as the operator of the Microsoft Store) and GitHub (as the platform where we host downloads) each handle the information they collect under their own privacy statements.

7. Data retention

We do not keep personal information about users of our Apps or Website on our own systems. Aggregated reports from Partner Center and GitHub are retained for as long as they are useful for maintaining and improving the Apps. If you contact us by email, we keep your message only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping.

8. Security

Because our Apps and Website do not collect personal information ourselves, there is little for us to store or protect. We take reasonable steps to safeguard any information we do receive, such as emails sent to us directly.

9. Children's privacy

Our Apps and Website are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in some regions), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal information about you through our Apps or Website, we usually will not have anything to provide or delete. For other services connected through our domain, follow the rights process in that service's own privacy policy. To exercise rights regarding information held by Microsoft, please use Microsoft's privacy tools at account.microsoft.com/privacy or contact Microsoft directly. For information held by GitHub, such as your GitHub account, use GitHub's own privacy settings or contact GitHub. You may also contact us at the address below and we will do our best to help.

11. International users

Our Apps are available worldwide through the Microsoft Store and GitHub. Microsoft and GitHub may process information in countries other than your own, under the terms of their own privacy statements. We do not transfer personal information across borders ourselves through the Apps or Website.

12. Other services connected to our domain

Our domain, lukeit.net, and its subdomains may connect you to services other than the Website and the Apps, including services we run ourselves and external services provided by others. This policy does not cover them, because they work differently and collect different information. Each has its own privacy policy, which applies instead of this one for that service. If you use our Apps or Website as well as another service connected to our domain, both policies apply to you, each for its own service. If a service connected to our domain is run by someone else, we are not responsible for its privacy practices, so please read its own privacy policy.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top and post the new version on our Website. Material changes will also be reflected in our Microsoft Store listings and GitHub repository where appropriate.
